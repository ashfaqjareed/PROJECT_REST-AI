Complete WhatsApp Order Journey

From Customer Greeting to Kitchen Expeditor to Ready for Pickup

Step 01: Identification & Warm Greeting

Customer Names & Greets

The customer scans the table/flyer QR code or taps your WhatsApp link. They provide their name first. Ordera Bot immediately greets them personally: "Hi [Name]! How are you today? Welcome to our restaurant!" and serves the interactive live digital food menu instantly.

Hi, my name is Nuwan!
Hello Nuwan! 😊 How are you today? Here is our fresh menu. What would you like to order today?
Step 02: Smart Menu Selection

Chooses Menu & Modifiers

The guest selects their desired food dishes (e.g. 2x Chicken Kottu, extra cheese, spicy gravy). Ordera Bot validates pricing, calculates subtotal, taxes, and automatically checks inventory availability.

I would like 2 Chicken Kottu with extra spicy gravy.
Got it! 2x Chicken Kottu added with extra spicy gravy. (Total: Rs. 2,000). Table #4.
Step 03: Review, Edit & Final Confirmation

Flexible Item Update or Lock-In

Before placing the ticket, the AI explicitly asks the customer if they wish to add items, modify spice levels, delete anything, or confirm. Once the guest confirms, the ticket is finalized with an automated itemized summary receipt.

Would you like to add beverages, delete anything, or confirm your order?
All looks great, confirm please! ✅
🎉 Confirmed! Ticket #204 locked in.
Step 04: Manager & Owner Relay

Instant Manager Approval & Sync

The moment the customer confirms, the order is dispatched in real-time to the manager's terminal/WhatsApp dashboard. The manager verifies special requests and instantly routes the ticket to the kitchen station.

MANAGER DASHBOARD • UPLINK ACTIVE
Incoming Order #204: Nuwan (Table 4)
Verified & Dispatched to Kitchen Display System (KDS) →
Step 05: Kitchen Prep & One-Click Readiness

Kitchen Reads & Hits 'Ready'

The kitchen crew receives the digital KOT on their kitchen display device. Once the food is cooked and plated, the chef simply taps the 'Food Ready' button on their screen.

KITCHEN TOUCH TERMINAL
Order #204: 2x Chicken Kottu
✔ Click: Food Is Ready
Step 06: Automated Customer Pickup Notification

Guest Alerted to Pickup

The instant the kitchen marks the dish as ready, the Ordera Bot AI API fires an immediate WhatsApp notification directly to the customer: "Nuwan, your piping hot food is ready! Please come and pick it up from Counter A."

🔔 Order Ready! Hi Nuwan, your food is fresh and ready. Please come and pick up your meal at Counter A!
Awesome, heading over now! Thank you! 🏃‍♂️
